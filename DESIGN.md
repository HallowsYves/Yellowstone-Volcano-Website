# Design System Theme
This document outlines the core aesthetic principles and token values that define our design system.
## Color Palette
Our theme embraces a **dark** mode, providing a sophisticated and comfortable viewing experience.

 

*   **Primary Color:** The brand's most distinctive chromatic color is derived from **#FFD700**. This bold golden hue is suitable for buttons, calls-to-action, and key interactive elements, reflecting our commitment to impactful user engagement.

*   **Neutral Color:** A deep **#1A1A1A** serves as our primary neutral base color, providing a strong foundation for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic elements in our dark theme.

 

## Typography

 

Our typographic system is designed for clarity and impact, using a curated selection of font families.

 

*   **Headlines:** Crafted with **Noto Serif**, our headlines carry a sense of timeless elegance and authority.

*   **Body Text:** Our body text utilizes **Public Sans**, chosen for its excellent legibility and modern, clean aesthetic, ensuring comfortable reading across all content.

*   **Labels:** Labels also employ **Public Sans**, providing consistency and clarity for interactive elements and data display.
## Shape and Form
The geometry of our UI elements contributes to the overall feel and user perception.
*   **Roundedness:** UI shapes maintain a **sharp, angular (0)** quality, emphasizing precision and a modern, crisp aesthetic.
## Spacing
The arrangement and proximity of UI elements are carefully managed to create a balanced and intuitive layout.
*   **Spacing:** A **normal (2)** level of whitespace is applied, ensuring a comfortable balance between information density and visual breathing room.
