# Yellowstone Site Content

## Section 1: Introduction
Yellowstone is a caldera-forming supervolcano located in Wyoming, fueled by a deep mantle plume. It is part of a volcanic track created as the North American plate moves southwest.

## Section 2: Eruption History
The system has seen three massive eruptions over the last 2.1 million years:
* **Huckleberry Ridge (2.1 Ma):** 2,450 cubic km of ash.
* **Mesa Falls (1.3 Ma):** 280 cubic km of ash.
* **Lava Creek (640,000 years ago):** 1,000 cubic km of ash, which created the current caldera.

## Section 3: Current Activity
Yellowstone is a restless system monitored by the Yellowstone Volcano Observatory (YVO).
* **Seismicity:** 1,500 to 3,000 earthquakes annually.
* **Hydrothermal:** Over 10,000 features like geysers and hot springs.
* **Current Status:** The alert level as of early 2026 is NORMAL/GREEN.
